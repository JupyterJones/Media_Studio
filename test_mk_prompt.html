"The smiling car looked happy."
"The fox wore clothes and talked."
Pareidolia 👁️ — seeing meaningful patterns, especially faces, where none actually exist.
Seeing a face in the front of a car. Seeing a face in the clouds or on the Moon.
Anthropomorphic — the adjective describing something that has human-like form or qualities.
"An anthropomorphic cat." "An anthropomorphic robot."
If you're talking specifically about finding human faces or human body shapes hidden in ordinary objects or animals, the best term is usually pareidolia. If you're talking about designing an animal or object so it looks or behaves like a person, the correct term is anthropomorphism (or saying the object is anthropomorphic).

"Anthropomorphic wolf" → a wolf with a human body, posture, or clothing.
