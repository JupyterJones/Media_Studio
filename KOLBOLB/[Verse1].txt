For that mystical Celtic + cosmic sound, I’d use D minor.

Key: D minor

It gives you:

🌙 A dark, mystical quality
✨ Enough emotional space for a clear female vocal
🪕 Good compatibility with Celtic melodies
🌌 A natural “ancient/otherworldly” feeling
🎹 Works very well with atmospheric synth pads and drones

For a slightly more haunting and ethereal version, try E minor. It tends to feel more open and soaring, especially for female vocals.

For your particular lyrics and chorus, I would start with D minor, around 78–84 BPM.
