🎵 AceStep Music Quick Reference

Here are the optimal default values for your AMD Ryzen 5 CPU setup:

Setting Recommended Value Notes
BPM 120 Standard upbeat tempo (use 90 for slow ambient)
Duration (s) 25s (or 55s) 1 Verse: 25s. 2 Verses: Check <TwoVerses> (sets 55s & generates both verse codes).
Key A Minor Dark sci-fi mood (or C Major for bright)
Time Sig 4 Standard 4/4 meter (integer value)
Lyrics Verses / Chorus For Singing Vocals: Put rhyming words with [Verse] & [Chorus].
For No Vocals: Put [instrumental].
🎤 Why There Was No Voice Before:
The default template had [instrumental] in the Lyrics box! In AceStep, [instrumental] is the special AI tag that commands the engine to silence all singing and produce purely background instruments.

To Hear Singing Vocals:
  1. Replace [instrumental] with actual lyrics lines (e.g. [Verse 1] and [Chorus]).
  2. Add vocal keywords in the Caption (e.g., "melodic synthwave, female vocals, clear emotional singing").
  3. Set Duration to 25s or 30s so the singer has time to sing the lines!
💡 Magic Shortcut: You can also just type a style description in Caption and click [ Plan Lyrics ]. The AI will write full rhyming lyrics, calculate BPM, Key, and Duration automatically!
