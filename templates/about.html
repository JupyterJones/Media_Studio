Kokoro Neural TTS, Speech Synthesis & Audio Studio

High-performance offline neural speech engine powered by Kokoro ONNX on CPU. Supports precise millisecond pause injection, multi-character screenplay tags, 50 voices, and instant lipsync staging.

11 Functions & 50 Voices
⚡ 15×–20× Real-Time CPU 📦 Zero Docker Overhead 🎯 Millisecond Pause Accuracy 🎭 50 Neural Voice Vectors 💾 Self-Contained Local Weights 👄 Instant Avatar Lipsync Staging
Native Kokoro ONNX Architecture: Media Studio features an offline, CPU-optimized speech generation pipeline running directly on AMD Ryzen via ONNX Runtime without container latency or CUDA GPU locks. Weights are fully self-contained in models/kokoro/kokoro-v1.0.onnx (311 MB) and models/kokoro/voices-v1.0.bin (25 MB). The synthesis engine operates 100% in RAM—evaluating screenplay markup, generating float32 audio buffers, inserting exact NumPy silence gaps, applying volume gain, preventing digital clipping, and exporting pristine 192k MP3s directly to static/voice/ and static/projects/use.mp3 for 1-click Wav2Lip avatar lipsync.

⚡ Production Script Markup Cheatsheet

Embed these production tags anywhere in your dialogue or narration script in /text_to_mp3:

[voice=NAME] Speaker Cast

Switches the speaker persona for this sentence or line. Allows multiple characters to hold realistic multi-turn conversations in a single rendered MP3.

[voice=am_adam]Welcome to the series.[voice=af_sarah]I'm excited to explore with you.
[pause=SECONDS] Precision Silence

Inserts exact floating-point digital silence between phrases or at line ends. Can also be placed alone on a line for dramatic scene pauses without speech.

[pause=1.5] (1.5s gap) • [pause=0.5] (500ms) • [pause=2.25] (scene change)
[slow] / [fast] Tempo & Speed

Controls delivery pacing. [slow] delivers at 0.85× calm speed for thoughtful moments; [fast] speaks at 1.15× energetic rate. Or use exact [speed=0.95].

[slow]We dive into ancient mysteries.[fast]Things are moving rapidly!
[loud] / [soft] Acoustic Inflection

Modulates acoustic energy. [loud] amplifies audio by +4dB (1.5× boost) with soft-clipping safety; [soft] drops volume to a gentle whisper (-4dB / 0.6×).

[loud]BE CAREFUL![soft]Shh, don't wake the guardians.
[whisper] Dramatic Whisper

Combines intimate volume reduction (0.45× amplitude) with relaxed speaking tempo (0.85× speed) for secrets, dramatic suspense, and confessionals.

[whisper]Did you see what just moved in the dark ruins?[pause=1.0]
Regex Normalization Zero Audio Leak

All bracketed markup [...] is automatically stripped by regex before text passes to Kokoro. The synthesized speech never reads brackets aloud.

Input: [voice=am_adam]Hello[pause=1.0] → Speaks: "Hello" + 1.0s silence
