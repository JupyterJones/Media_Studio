ℹ️ Keyframe Guide & Parameters

Each item in the JSON array represents a camera keyframe:

  • zoom: Zoom factor (e.g. 1.0 is unzoomed 100%, 2.0 is 2x zoom).
  • pan: [x, y] coordinates from 0.0 (top-left) to 1.0 (bottom-right).
  • Transitions between adjacent keyframes are smoothly interpolated with sub-pixel bicubic warping and cosine easing curve to remove sharp turns or jitter.
  • The output video is sharpened with an unsharp mask and optionally styled with the standard 512x768 border frame.
